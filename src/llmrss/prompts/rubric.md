- novelty: is it truly new, or a variation of something known?
- impact: does it change cost, capability or scale by orders of magnitude? Does it open a field?
- evidence: is there a paper, demo, code or data, or only an announcement?
- hype (penalty): marketing, superlatives without data, news-cycle churn.
Value what "looks like a weird toy but actually works", and what shows up in several
independent sources.
